Introduction
============
This document contains sample PDS label files for each of the different data file
types archive by the MAVEN SWIA instrument. This includes:

   A. Coarse Survey 3-D Ion Distributions
   B. Coarse Archive 3-D Ion Distributions
   C. Fine Survey 3-D Ion Distributions
   D. Fine Archvie 3-D Ion Distributions
   E. Onboard Survey Ion Moments
   F. Onboard Survey Ion Energy Spectra
   

A. Coarse Survey 3-D Ion Distributions

This sample label is from the MAVEN SWIA coarse resolution survey telemetry rate 
3-D ion distribution collection 
(LID=urn:nasa:pds:maven.swia.calibrated:data.coarse_svy_3d). The sample data 
product label is for the date 2015-01-02.
