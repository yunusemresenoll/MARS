Mars Atmosphere and Volatile EvolutioN (MAVEN) 
Solar Wind Ion Analyzer (SWIA)
Calibrated PDS Archive Bundle
LID = urn:nasa:pds:maven.swia.calibrated

Bundle Description
==================

Overview
--------
The MAVEN SWIA calibrated PDS archive bundle contains fully calibrated SWIA 
data, including ion velocity distributions, energy spectra, and density, 
temperature, and velocity moments from onboard calculations. The data files 
include tables of sensitivity and energy/angle maps are included. Data consist 
of Coarse and Fine resolution 3d distributions and energy spectra and moments 
from onboard computations. The Coarse and Fine resolution data come in two 
forms, termed "Survey" and "Archive" The Survey data nominally represents data 
with lower resolution (time, angle, and/or energy) but complete temporal 
coverage. The Archive data nominally represents data with higher resolution 
(time, angle, and/or energy), but incomplete temporal coverage, selected by 
ground command to provide better characterization of "interesting" time 
periods, based on human selection of time intervals using the Survey data.

Data Processing Notes
=====================

Data Timing Uncertainty
-----------------------
Some of the data files in this bundle may extend beyond the time for which the 
Spacecraft Clock (SCLK) drift rate was defined based upon the SPICE MAVEN SCLK 
kernel used to process them. As a result the SCLK drift rate may have been 
predicted, resulting in small errors in the time (typically less than a few 
1/100's seconds). Where this may be the case a note has been included in the 
individual PDS product labels.

The arrays in some of the data files may contain occasional values that exceed
the valid maximum value for that array.


Bundle Contents
===============

Collections
-----------
This bundle includes the following collections:

   * Coarse Survey 3D - Full 3D ion distributions in units of differential 
     energy flux from SWIA coarse survey data
     (LID = urn:nasa:pds:maven.swia.calibrated:data.coarse_svy_3d)

   * Coarse Archive 3D - Full 3d ion distributions in units of differential 
     energy flux from SWIA coarse archive data
     (LID = urn:nasa:pds:maven.swia.calibrated:data.coarse_arc_3d)

   * Fine Survey 3D - Full 3d ion distributions in units of differential 
     energy flux from SWIA fine survey data
     (LID = urn:nasa:pds:maven.swia.calibrated:data.fine_svy_3d)

   * Fine Archive 3D - 	Full 3d ion distributions in units of differential 
     energy flux from SWIA fine archive data
     (LID = urn:nasa:pds:maven.swia.calibrated:data.fine_arc_3d)

   * Onboard Survey Moments - Ion density, temperature, and velocity moments 
     in physical units and coordinates calculated onboard from SWIA coarse or 
     fine data (depending on mode)
     (LID = urn:nasa:pds:maven.swia.calibrated:data.onboard_svy_mom)

   * Onboard Survey Spectra - Ion energy spectra in units of differential 
     energy flux calculated onboard from SWIA coarse data
     (LID = urn:nasa:pds:maven.swia.calibrated:data.onboard_svy_spec)

Data File Naming
----------------
Data files in this bundle are named according to the following convention:

   mvn_swi_l2_<desc>_<yyyy><mm><dd>_v<xx>_r<yy>.cdf
   
where:

   <desc>   = A descriptor used to identify the data file type
   <yyyy>   = 4-digit year of data coverage date
   <mm>     = 2-digit month of year of data coverage date
   <dd>     = 2-digit day of month of data coverage date
   <xx>     = 2-digit software version
   <yy>     = 2-digit data file version under the specified software version
